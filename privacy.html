Privacy policy

Website Page Auditor · Last updated: October 14, 2026

Website Page Auditor has a single purpose: audit the webpage you're viewing and help you find and review quality issues. This policy explains what the extension reads, where that information goes, and which network requests it makes. It describes the extension's actual behavior; if the behavior changes, this policy is updated first.

Summary

  • The extension reads a webpage only after you agree to the in-product notice, and only on the tab where you use it.
  • Findings stay in your browser. They are never sent to the developer or to any other server.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code.
  • Data is not sold, transferred to third parties, or used for any purpose other than showing you the audit.

What the extension reads

When you check a page, open the Search appearance tab or use an Inspect page tool, the extension reads the following from that page (Chrome Web Store category: website content):

  • the page address (URL) and title;
  • visible text, the HTML structure, computed styles and element sizes and positions;
  • links, images and their addresses, and meta tags such as og:image;
  • form field labels, types and validation settings.

It does not read what you type into form fields, passwords, cookies, browsing history, or other tabs. The optional interactive form check enters its own sample values and then restores the original ones; neither the sample nor the original values are stored.

Network requests

The extension has no server of its own. Some checks make requests to the website you're auditing or to sites it links to:

FeatureDefaultWhat is requested
Same-site link statusOnLightweight HEAD requests (GET as a fallback) from the page to links on the same site, up to the limit in Settings. They come from the page, so they include your usual session for that site. Logout, delete, cart and download links are skipped.
Images check and image previewsPart of the Images checkImages the page already references may be loaded again to measure them, and the dashboard shows thumbnails of them. The og:image on the Search appearance tab is loaded to measure its size, without a referrer.
External linksSelected by defaultCookie-free HEAD requests to other sites the page links to. Requires permission to access all sites; links are not requested without it.
Test search queryOnSubmits the query you set in Settings to the audited site's own search. It may navigate the page to its results.
Interactive form validationOnNo requests: sample values are entered and the submit button pressed so the site's validation runs, while real submission and data-sending requests are blocked.
Tabs and keyboard focusOnTemporarily opens and closes tabs or disclosure widgets and moves keyboard focus to check its visibility.

Your choices

  • Choose “Not now” on the notice, and the extension won't read any page.
  • Delete a tab's report with “Delete this report”.
  • Withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy. All stored reports are deleted, and the notice is shown again before the next audit.
  • Remove the extension to delete its settings.

Limits of the audit

Automated checks find common problems on the page as it is at that moment. They don't test a whole site, don't guarantee accessibility or compliance with any standard, and don't replace human review.
