Before you audit a page

Website Page Auditor audits the webpage you're viewing and helps you find and review quality issues. To do that, it has to read the page you choose to check.

  • What it reads: the page address and title, visible text, HTML structure, styles and element sizes, links, images, and form field labels and settings. It does not read what you type into form fields, passwords or cookies.
  • When: only on the tab where you open it, and only when you check the page, open the Search appearance tab or use an Inspect page tool.
  • Where findings go: they stay in this browser's temporary storage for that tab and are deleted when the tab closes or moves to another page. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server.
  • PDF report: findings you add to the PDF report, with a screenshot of each highlighted element, stay in this browser's temporary storage until you clear the list or close the browser, even after the tab moves to another page.
  • Optional checks: on by default; change them in Settings. UI tests briefly open and close tabs. Form tests use sample values but block submissions. Search sends a real query and may navigate. Same-site links use your session; external link checks require permission to access all sites.
